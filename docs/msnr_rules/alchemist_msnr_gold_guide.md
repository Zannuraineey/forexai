# The Alchemist MSNR & SMT Playbook for Gold (XAUUSD) & Silver (XAGUSD)

Based on *AbayFX / Berk Usta 100-Page Malaysian Support and Resistance (MSNR) & Alchemist Playbook*.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

The **Alchemist MSNR (Malaysian Support and Resistance)** framework treats market prices as governed by an institutional "Storyline":
1. **Price Never Moves Randomly**: Price moves systematically from one Higher Timeframe (HTF: D1/H4) liquidity pool/key level to another.
2. **Internal vs External Liquidity (IRL <-> ERL)**:
   - **Internal Range Liquidity (IRL)**: Fair Value Gaps (FVG) and Order Blocks (OB) inside the range.
   - **External Range Liquidity (ERL)**: Swing Highs (Buy-Side Liquidity - BSL) and Swing Lows (Sell-Side Liquidity - SSL).
3. **Execution Edge**: Entries take place on Lower Timeframes (M15/M5) inside HTF zones, targeting 50% Consequent Encroachment (CE) with confirmed Market Structure Shift (MSS) and SMT Divergence.

## 2. Core Building Blocks of MSNR

### A. Classic SNR Levels
- **Classic A (Peak)**: Resistance formed at swing highs (inverted 'V' shape).
- **Classic V (Trough)**: Support formed at swing lows ('V' shape).

### B. Flip Structures (RBS & SBR)
- **RBS (Resistance Becomes Support)**:
  - Price impulsively breaks above a Classic A Resistance.
  - Price returns to test the broken level.
  - Validated when price taps the level and rejects with a bullish confirmation candle.
- **SBR (Support Becomes Resistance)**:
  - Price impulsively breaks below a Classic V Support.
  - Price returns to test the broken level from underneath.
  - Validated when price taps the level and rejects with a bearish confirmation candle.

### C. Zone Freshness & Quality Rating
- **Fresh Zone (0-1 prior touches)**: Premium quality (Highest win rate).
- **Tested Zone (2 touches)**: Moderate quality.
- **Exhausted Zone (3+ touches)**: Low quality; high risk of breakout/liquidity run.

## 3. Order Block & Consequent Encroachment (CE 50%)

The Alchemist playbook identifies the exact institutional entry point as the **50% midpoint** of the reaction candle:

### Bullish Consequent Encroachment:
$$\text{CE}_{\text{Bullish}} = \frac{\text{Open} + \text{Low}}{2} \quad \text{or} \quad \frac{\text{High} + \text{Low}}{2} \text{ (Wick CE)}$$

### Bearish Consequent Encroachment:
$$\text{CE}_{\text{Bearish}} = \frac{\text{Open} + \text{High}}{2} \quad \text{or} \quad \frac{\text{High} + \text{Low}}{2} \text{ (Wick CE)}$$

- **Optimal Entry**: Limit or market execution when price retraces to touch the 50% CE level of the MSNR zone.
- **Invalidation (Stop Loss)**: Beyond the wick extreme of the SNR / Order Block zone.

## 5. Daily Profile #2: New York Reversal + XAUUSD / XAGUSD SMT Divergence

Slide 100 specifically details the **Daily Profile #2 (New York Reversal)**:
1. **Setup Phase**: During the London session or pre-NY session, price pushes toward key MSNR levels.
2. **Correlated Asset Discrepancy (SMT)**:
   - **XAUUSD vs XAGUSD**:
     - **Bullish SMT Divergence**: Silver (XAGUSD) sweeps below a prior swing low, but Gold (XAUUSD) makes a **Higher Low (HL)** and respects the MSNR Support / 50% CE level. Gold demonstrates institutional accumulation.
     - **Bearish SMT Divergence**: Silver sweeps above a prior swing high, but Gold makes a **Lower High (LH)** and respects the MSNR Resistance / 50% CE level. Gold demonstrates institutional distribution.
3. **Confirmation**:
   - Lower timeframe (M15/M5) Market Structure Shift (MSS) with an impulsive displacement candle.
   - Retest to the 50% CE of the MSNR zone.
4. **Execution Rules**:
   - **Entry**: Retest of 50% CE of MSNR zone during NY AM session (12:00 - 15:00 UTC / 7:00 - 10:00 AM EST).
   - **Stop Loss**: 1-2 pips beyond the displacement candle low/high or zone extreme.
   - **Target 1**: Opposing session liquidity (London/Asia high or low).
   - **Target 2**: Higher Timeframe External Range Liquidity (ERL).
   - **Minimum Risk-Reward**: 1:2.0 to 1:3.0.
